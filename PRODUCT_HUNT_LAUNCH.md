## 1. LISTING COPY

### Tagline (60 char max)
**"Paste a GitHub URL. Get ready-to-publish marketing content."**

Alt options:
- "Turn any GitHub repo into social posts, blogs, and case studies"
- "Your codebase writes its own marketing now"

### Description

Most developers build great tools but never write a single marketing post about them. LaunchCopy fixes that in 30 seconds.

**How it works:**
1. Paste any GitHub URL (public or private)
2. Our AI reads your README, file structure, dependencies, and metadata
3. You get platform-ready X posts, LinkedIn content, SEO blog articles, and case studies — all based on what your code actually does

**What makes it different:**
- Two-pass AI refinement: content is generated, then edited by a second AI pass that removes AI-sounding language, fact-checks against your repo, and scores quality
- Every piece gets a 4-axis quality score (Relevance, Engagement, Clarity, Humanness) so you know what's ready to ship
- Platform-native formatting: X posts respect the 280 char limit, LinkedIn posts use the right paragraph structure, blogs come with H2 headers and ~800 words
- Customize tone (casual/professional/playful/enterprise), audience, industry, and brand voice before generation
- Export as Markdown, JSON, copy as X thread, or copy as LinkedIn posts

**Who it's for:**
- Indie devs and open-source maintainers who hate writing marketing copy
- Startup founders who need content but don't have a marketing team
- DevRel engineers and developer advocates producing content at scale
- Agencies writing about software products for clients

**Pricing:** Free tier (1 scan, no signup needed to try) / Starter $10/mo / Pro $40/mo with unlimited scans and regeneration.

## 4. LAUNCH DAY PLAYBOOK

### Timing

- **Best day:** Tuesday, Wednesday, or Thursday
- **Go live:** Product Hunt launches at 12:01 AM PT. Have everything ready by 11:55 PM PT the night before.
- **Peak hours:** 6-10 AM PT is when most voters browse. Be active in comments during this window.

### T-minus Checklist

**1 week before:**
- [ ] Finalize all gallery screenshots (1270x760)
- [ ] Create OG image (1200x630)
- [ ] Schedule the launch on Product Hunt
- [ ] Write and save the maker's first comment
- [ ] Prepare a list of 20-30 people to DM on launch day
- [ ] Draft launch-day tweets (see section 5)
- [ ] Draft launch-day LinkedIn post (see section 5)

**1 day before:**
- [ ] Double-check listing copy, images, links
- [ ] Test the product — make sure a fresh user can paste a URL and get results
- [ ] Prepare email to your mailing list (if any)
- [ ] Set an alarm for 12:01 AM PT

**Launch day:**
- [ ] 12:01 AM PT — Confirm launch is live
- [ ] 12:05 AM PT — Post maker's first comment
- [ ] 6:00 AM PT — Send DMs to supporters
- [ ] 6:30 AM PT — Post on X
- [ ] 7:00 AM PT — Post on LinkedIn
- [ ] 7:30 AM PT — Share in relevant communities (see section 6)
- [ ] All day — Reply to EVERY comment within 30 minutes
- [ ] 12:00 PM PT — Second X post (results/traction update)
- [ ] 5:00 PM PT — Thank-you post on X

### Critical Rules

1. **Reply to every single comment.** This is the #1 factor in Product Hunt ranking.
2. **Never ask for upvotes.** Say "I'd love your feedback" or "Would mean a lot if you checked it out."
3. **Don't send direct links to the PH page** — Product Hunt penalizes direct-link traffic. Share your producthunt.com/posts/... URL naturally.
4. **Be genuine in comments.** Answer questions honestly. Admit limitations.
